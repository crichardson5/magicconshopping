Card-list JSON fields: deck, qty, name, note, and colorIdentity. Use color codes W, U, B, R, G in an array; leave colorIdentity null to load it from Scryfall with prices. Prices are the lowest Scryfall paper USD prices found for each card; they are indicative market prices, not verified active seller listings. Prices may be unavailable when the browser blocks API requests. Shipping, condition and availability are not included. The CSV includes all cards even if live prices fail.
